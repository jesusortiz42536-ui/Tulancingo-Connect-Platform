# Tulancingo Connect Platform

A hyper-local digital ecosystem built for the people of **Tulancingo, Hidalgo, Mexico**. The platform integrates community commerce (Ventul), professional logistics (Cargo-GO), and civic engagement (Denarytor) into a single, secure, and open-source environment — empowering the local economy and strengthening community bonds.

---

## 🏘️ Social Impact in Tulancingo

Tulancingo is a vibrant mid-sized city with a rich artisan tradition, a growing SME sector, and an active civil society. Yet residents and local businesses often face barriers that large, national platforms fail to address: high transaction fees, impersonal services, lack of local delivery infrastructure, and limited channels for civic participation.

The Tulancingo Connect Platform directly tackles these challenges:

- **Economic empowerment**: By connecting buyers and sellers through Ventul, local merchants — from street vendors to established SMEs — gain access to a digital marketplace that keeps revenue flowing within the community.
- **Logistics equity**: Cargo-GO provides affordable last-mile delivery infrastructure, reducing dependence on costly national couriers and creating jobs for local couriers and carriers.
- **Civic voice**: Denarytor gives every resident a structured, respectful space to discuss neighborhood issues, propose solutions, and participate in local decision-making — strengthening trust between citizens and local government.

Together, these three modules form a resilient, community-owned digital infrastructure that reflects Tulancingo's identity and serves its 250,000+ residents.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Each module contains its own `README.md` with setup instructions. Start by exploring the module most relevant to your contribution:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/jesusortiz42536-ui/Tulancingo-Connect-Platform.git
cd Tulancingo-Connect-Platform

# Navigate to a module
cd logistics_engine   # Cargo-GO
cd marketplace_api    # Ventul
cd civic_forum        # Denarytor
```
